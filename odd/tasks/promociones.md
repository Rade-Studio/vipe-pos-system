## Specs

Phase 1 — engine and data (DB + API)
- S1 (L1, 01 §1–2): a promotion has `kind` one of `product_discount` (percentage or fixed amount per unit, as today), `buy_x_pay_y` (`buyQuantity` N ≥ 2, `payQuantity` M with 1 ≤ M < N, `restDiscountPercent` 1–100, 100 = free: "2x1", "3x2", "Segunda unidad al 50%"), `combo` (`comboPrice` > 0 and 1–6 groups, each with a name and ≥ 1 dish option, pick exactly 1 per group: "Menú ejecutivo $24.000") or `bill_discount` (percentage or fixed, optional `minSubtotal`, optional `maxDiscount`: "10% en domicilios desde $80.000", tope $15.000). Targets for product_discount and buy_x_pay_y are any mix of dishes, categories and subcategories (a dish matches if it is listed or is in a listed category/subcategory). Conditions, all optional: `startDate` (required) and `endDate` (nullable = no end), `daysOfWeek` (ISO 1 = Monday … 7 = Sunday; empty/null = every day), `timeWindows` [{ from "HH:MM", to "HH:MM" }] with from < to (empty = all day), `channels` subset of salón / para llevar / domicilio (empty = all), `maxUsesPerOrder` (nullable). `mode` is `automatic`, `manual` or `manual_pin`; manual modes never apply by themselves. `active=false` is "Pausada". Times are evaluated in the restaurant's timezone. Existing promotions migrate to `product_discount`, `automatic`, every day, all day, all channels, with their current dish links as targets; nothing else about them changes.
- S2 (01 R4): the list price is never rewritten. `order_items.price` stores the unit list price (options included). Every discount is its own record with order, optional order item, optional promotion, kind, a label snapshot (the promotion name, e.g. "Happy hour 2x1"), amount (whole pesos, > 0), and for manual ones `reason`, `created_by` and `authorized_by`. `orders.total_discounts` = sum of the order's discounts; `orders.subtotal` stays the net base (Σ price·qty − total_discounts) so tax, tip, total and `pay_order` keep their meaning; the API also returns `grossSubtotal` = subtotal + totalDiscounts. A discount never exceeds its line value, and the order's discounts never exceed the gross subtotal. Historical orders are not rewritten.
- S3 (01 R1, R2, L3): automatic product promotions are fixed when the items are sent, i.e. when the server inserts them: the server evaluates with its own clock in the restaurant timezone and the order's channel, and each inserted item stores the promotion it qualified for together with a snapshot of the rule. Editing, pausing or ending a promotion afterwards does not change items already sent (01 R7). When several promotions qualify for an item, the one that saves the customer the most on that item wins (buy_x_pay_y counts as price·(N−M)/N·restDiscountPercent/100); ties keep the oldest promotion. buy_x_pay_y pools all non-voided units of the order tagged with the same promotion, across sends and across different dishes, sorts them by unit list price descending, and in each consecutive group of N units the N−M cheapest get `restDiscountPercent` off (example from 01: Margarita $26.000 + Mojito $24.000 → "Happy hour 2x1 (Mojito) −$24.000"; 2 Mojito + 1 Margarita → −$24.000). `maxUsesPerOrder` caps how many units/groups get the discount. Percentages round to whole pesos per unit.
- S4 (01 R3): after product discounts, at most one automatic `bill_discount` applies, computed on the gross subtotal minus product discounts, only while that base ≥ `minSubtotal`, capped by `maxDiscount` (example: base $96.000 → "10% domicilio (tope $15.000) −$9.600"; base $103.000 → −$10.300). It is evaluated on every change to the order with the current time and channel, and removed when the order no longer qualifies.
- S5 (04): a combo is ordered explicitly: the client sends one combo line naming the promotion and one chosen dish per group; the server validates the promotion is active now for the channel and that each choice belongs to its group (422 otherwise), inserts each chosen dish as its own order item (so kitchen and bar receive "Sopa", "Bandeja", "Jugo" as today) linked by a shared combo id, and records one discount = Σ list prices − comboPrice (example: $9.000 + $17.000 + $7.000 → "Menú ejecutivo −$9.000"; if the sum is ≤ comboPrice no discount is recorded). Voiding any component voids the whole combo.
- S6 (01 R5): voiding items recomputes the order's automatic discounts (buy_x_pay_y regroups the remaining units; bill discount re-evaluated). Example from 03: voiding 1 of 2 Mojitos while a Margarita sent at the same time remains keeps −$24.000 and the total goes from $103.000 to $79.000. The void flow can ask for a preview of the resulting discounts before confirming.
- S7 (01 R6, 03): the cashier can add a manual discount to an item or to the order: either a promotion with mode `manual`/`manual_pin` (e.g. "Cortesía de cumpleaños", 1 por cuenta) or a free discount (percentage or fixed). It requires a reason (3–200 characters, trimmed) and, for `manual_pin` and for free discounts, the PIN authorization used by voids. Manual discounts are kept by every recompute and can be removed by the cashier (same authorization). They appear with who authorized them.
- S8 (API surface): the Order and OrderItem responses carry their discounts (`id`, `kind`, `label`, `amount`, `promotionId`, `orderItemId`, `reason`, `authorizedBy {id, name}`), plus `grossSubtotal` and `totalDiscounts`. `POST /orders/pricing-preview` takes a channel, an optional order id and draft lines (dishes with quantity and options, and combo lines), and returns, as if sent now, the lines with their discounts, the bill discount, totals, and hints: buy_x_pay_y groups missing units ("Falta 1 cóctel para otro 2x1."), minutes until the active window ends, and the amount missing for a bill discount minimum ("Domicilio: faltan $X para el 10% de descuento."). A promotions endpoint for terminals returns, for a channel, the promotions active now (with the end time of the current window), later today (with the start time) and the others with their rule. The waiter keeps sending `unitPrice`, now compared with the list price.

Tabs "Todas", "Aplicando ahora", "Programadas", "Pausadas", "Manuales", "Terminadas" with counts. - S10: the editor asks, in order, "¿Qué beneficio da?" (the four kinds), "¿Sobre qué productos?" (fields per kind as in 02, including combo groups and bill minimum and cap), "¿Cuándo?" (days, from/to, valid from/until, help "Cuenta la hora en que se pide cada producto, no la hora del cobro."), "¿Dónde y con qué límites?" Phase 3 — service (03, 04)
- S11 (04): the waiter's order screen shows the active promotion strip (name, the customer sentence and "quedan N min" / "hasta HH:MM"; yellow when ≤ 10 min: "Termina a las 19:00. Envía el pedido antes para que se aplique."; "Sin promociones en este momento" otherwise), "2x1 · hasta 19:00" / "2x1 desde 17:00" tags on category, subcategory and dish tiles, per-line discounts and "Ahorro con promociones" in the order panel from the pricing preview, the nudge "Falta 1 cóctel para otro 2x1." with quick-add buttons, the warning "Envía antes de las 19:00." when the window ends in ≤ 10 min with unsent eligible items, sent lines showing "Happy hour 2x1 · fijado HH:MM", and a "Promociones" button opening the sheet "Promociones de hoy" (Ahora / Más tarde hoy / No aplican ahora en el salón, each with "Al cliente:" sentence). Kitchen and bar tickets show no prices or discounts.
- S12 (04): the "Almuerzo ejecutivo" (combo) tile opens the builder "Elige 1 de cada grupo" with groups, "Suelto $X" per option, "Por separado $X · ahorra $Y" and "Agregar menú · $24.000"; outside its schedule the tile is disabled with its schedule and a toast "El Menú ejecutivo se vende de lunes a viernes de 12:00 a 15:00".
- S13 (03): the cashier shows each line at list price with its discount lines (name, "fijado HH:MM" for automatic, who authorized for manual), "Subtotal (precio de lista)", one row per discount, base and tax, total; "Agregar cortesía o descuento" opens the sheet with promotion or free discount, product, "Motivo" and PIN, "Autorizar y aplicar"; voiding an item shows the recomputed discounts before confirming ("Si anulas 1 Mojito, el 2x1 se recalcula.").
- S14 (03): the invoice (browser print and ESC/POS) prints each discount as its own line under its item with the promotion name, a "Descuentos" total, and "Hoy ahorraste $X" when discounts > 0.
