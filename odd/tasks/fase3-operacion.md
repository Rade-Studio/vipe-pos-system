# Fase 3: ready for real restaurants

Make VIPE POS usable as the real system of a restaurant: audited cancellations, configurable waiter access, an optional print station with per-station routing, priced modifiers, moving/merging/splitting checks, DIAN e-invoicing through Factus, a complete cash close, reports, a professional PWA and resilience to bad networks. Accounting is out of scope. Work lands on `dev` (staging) in chained PRs; production is not touched.

## Specs

- S1 (L1, scope and order): "necesito solucionar todo, en el orden que tu recomiendas, el tema de la factura DIAN, hagamoslo con Factus como lo hicimos con mapanetbpo, cosa a tener en cuenta quizas. Temas de entrada rapida con PIN sea por configuracion del restaurante, ya que hay restaurantes que si van a dar cada uno un acceso a cada mesero, y otro que si puede ser un toma pedido compartido que puedan escoger una de las 2 en configuracion, no quiero nada de avisos al mesero, eso se maneja en el restaurante fisicamente, necesito es todo del 1 al 10, sin que falta absolutamente nada con los detalles que te mencione." The ten items (L1 b) minus waiter notices, in this order: (a) audited cancellations and voids with reason, actor and supervisor authorization, nothing is deleted; (b) waiter access mode per restaurant: own accounts or shared order-taking terminal with a personal PIN; (c) optional print station + kitchen/bar/cashier routing; (d) modifiers with price (options, sides, combos, half portions); (e) move an order to another table, merge tables, move items between checks, split by people or items; (f) DIAN e-invoicing through Factus; (g) complete cash close (denomination count, Z report, tips per waiter); (h) reports (sales by dish, hour and waiter, food cost, theoretical vs real consumption); (i) professional PWA with branded loading; (j) resilience to bad networks (S3).
- S2 (L2, printing is optional): "La intencion es que si tengan un computador fijo, ya que ahi vamos a configurar las impresoras termicas en caso que las tengan y eso tambien es opcional, hay restaurantes que no van a tener impresoras termicas, asi que puede que no necesiten imprimir y manejan todo por el sistema." A restaurant without printers works fully in the browser: kitchen screen for comandas, on-screen pre-check, digital invoice (Factus PDF / public link / QR, shareable by WhatsApp or email). A restaurant setting says whether printing is used; when it is not, no print action is offered.
- S3 (L3, networks): "si, asi me sirve" — Level 0 now: the cloud stays the single source of truth; every device keeps an outbox for orders, added items and comandas with idempotency keys, retries with backoff, and shows its connection state on every screen; disconnection minutes are measured per restaurant. Level 1 (the fixed computer as the only authority while offline, one-way replay) is NOT built unless the measurements justify it. Context: "la ultima vez que intente hacer el sistema offline first ... fue un desastre la sincronizacion por estructuras de red malas con perdidas de paquetes".
- S4 (L4, print station): "necesito crear otro sistema para instalar en algun computador que maneje todas las impresoras, el que tenemos me parece ineficiente y no lo entiendo" and "me hagas primero el diseño de la aplicacion de escritorio". A new optional desktop app (Tauri) designed first in design/estacion-vipe/; it replaces the Python listener in pos/.
- S5 (L5, out of scope): "Despues pensamos en la contabilidad que es mucho ams complejo". No accounting in this phase.
- S6 (quality bar, carried from odd/tasks/rediseno-pos.md S6 and S8): every screen works in light and dark at 390/768/1024/1440 without overflow with ≥ 44 px targets, verified on the running app; no invented data.

- L6 (decision, 2026-11-07): F2 auth design. Sessions: `user` (today's login, unchanged), `terminal` (POST /auth/terminal/login by an active admin or cashier; JWT claim `knd: terminal`; only the routes in apps/api/src/auth/terminal-access.ts, else 403 `terminal_route`), `acting` (POST /auth/identify {profileId, pin} on a terminal opens a 5-minute child session for an active admin, cashier or waiter with a PIN; its token, signed with a separate key, goes in `X-Vipe-Acting` and on the terminal's order writes swaps the request identity to that staff member, so RLS, role and `orders.waiter_id`/`created_by_profile_id` are theirs; it dies with its terminal). With `waiter_access_mode = shared_terminal` (business config `waiterAccessMode`, default `own_accounts`, admin writes, staff read) a terminal order write without a valid claim is 403 `acting_required`. PINs: 4-6 digits; one repeated digit, a run up or down (9 wraps to 0) or a repeated block are refused (Spanish 422); stored as argon2id of an HMAC-peppered PIN (pepper from JWT_SECRET: rotating it invalidates every PIN). Lockout: 5 consecutive failures lock the profile and the session that tried for 5 minutes; wrong PIN, unknown profile, other restaurant and no PIN answer the same 403 `pin_invalid`; locked answers 429 `pin_locked`. Supervisor authorization: POST /auth/authorize {pin, action, profileId?} with SUPERVISOR_ROLES = admin, cashier and actions cancel_order | void_item | void_payment returns a single-use id bound to the action and restaurant, valid 2 minutes; F1 consumes it with SupervisorAuthorizationsService.consume(id, action) (403 `authorization_invalid`). Admin sets/resets/removes PINs with PUT/DELETE /staff/:id/pin; a member changes their own with PUT /auth/pin {currentPin, pin}.
